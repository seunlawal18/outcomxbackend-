OutcomX · Build Roadmap

From wallet sign-in to real money moving

Eight milestones, in the order they depend on each other. Each one lists what it's for, what's blocking it, and exactly how to unblock it.

01

Wallet auth

Core verified

Wallet becomes the identity system — sign a message instead of a password. No domain-dependent email or Google login needed to launch.

What's left

  • Paste your Reown project ID into outcomx/.env.local as NEXT_PUBLIC_WALLETCONNECT_PROJECT_ID — MetaMask and Coinbase Wallet already work without it.
  • Check cloud.reown.com's dashboard for your plan's relay-usage limit — it only throttles the WalletConnect QR path, not MetaMask/Coinbase.
Scoped down for now: signatures are checked against a simple custom message, not the full EIP-4361 SIWE spec. Fine for sign-in; worth upgrading before Milestone 7 (withdrawals) touches real money.
02

Ledger hardening

Ready to start

Replace the single float balance column with an append-only double-entry ledger: integer micro-units instead of floats, refunds, idempotency keys — so money math can't silently drift.

What's needed from you

Nothing external. One decision when we get there: keep it USD-only for now (recommended) or lay groundwork for multi-currency entries up front.

03

Postgres migration

Ready to start

SQLite is fine for one dev machine; Postgres is needed once real money and more than one server process are involved. A Prisma schema already exists for this — currently unused.

What's needed from you

A hosted Postgres connection string. Pick one:

  • railway.app
  • supabase.com
  • neon.tech

How to get it

Free tier on any of the three, ~5 minutes: create a project, copy the connection string, paste it as DATABASE_URL in the backend's .env.

04

Real-time gateway

Ready to start

Replace the current 3s / 30s polling with push updates over Socket.IO, built on the events.ts emitter already scaffolded in the backend and currently unused.

What's needed from you

Nothing — say go whenever.

05

Crypto deposits

Needs 2 decisions

Users fund their ledger balance with real USDT/USDC.

What's needed from you

  • Chain. Ethereum mainnet gas fees will frustrate small deposits — Polymarket itself settles on Polygon. Recommend starting with one low-fee chain (Polygon or Base) rather than mainnet.
  • RPC provider to watch incoming deposits on-chain.

How to get it

Free signup at alchemy.com or infura.io → create an app for the chosen chain → copy the API key.

06

Card on-ramp

Needs domain

Let users buy in with a debit/credit card. A third-party widget handles KYC and the fiat→crypto conversion — OutcomX never touches fiat conversion directly, which avoids money-transmitter licensing.

What's needed from you

A business account with Transak or MoonPay, plus eventually a real domain (the widget needs an allowed origin to go live).

Sandbox keys are issued immediately after signup — the integration can be built and tested before the domain exists. Only going live needs the domain.
07

Withdrawals & treasury

Needs custody call

Let users cash back out. The highest-risk milestone — real money leaving the platform — needs an approval queue, per-user limits, and a hot/cold wallet split.

What's needed from you

A custody decision once volume exists to justify it: a single hot wallet you hold the key to is the simple starting point; a provider like Fireblocks or BitGo is the step up.

08

Production hardening

Needs domain + host

Secrets management, a rate-limit audit, monitoring/alerting, automated backups, a real deploy pipeline. The last step before real users touch real money.

What's needed from you

  • A domain — this also unblocks Google OAuth and email verification as fast-follows to Milestone 1 (the RESEND_API_KEY is already configured, just waiting on a domain to send from).
  • A hosting choice — Vercel is the natural fit for the Next.js frontend; Railway, Render, or Fly for the Express backend.
